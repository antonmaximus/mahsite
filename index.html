Quality Improvement and Innovation


IV Iron Utilization & Diagnostic Stewardship

University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine
January 2026 – Present

Lead a multidisciplinary quality improvement initiative to improve evidence-based outpatient IV iron utilization and referral workflows. The project applies Lean principles and iterative Plan–Do–Study–Act (PDSA) cycles to optimize diagnostic stewardship, standardize laboratory readiness, and improve patient access to infusion therapy. Selected for the American Society of Hematology (ASH) Quality Improvement Training Institute (QITI) to further develop and disseminate this work.

Highlights

  • Designed and implemented system-wide workflow interventions.
  • Led prospective data collection and statistical process control analysis.
  • Presented findings through the ASH QITI national program.
